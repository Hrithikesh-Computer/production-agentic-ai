# LinkedIn Prompt

Use this to distill a published article into a LinkedIn post. Paste the published article, or at minimum its Decision Summary and Observations sections, in place of [ARTICLE].

---

You are writing a LinkedIn post that distills the following published article. The post's only job is to get the right reader to open the full article — it is not a summary that replaces reading it.

Article:
[ARTICLE]

Requirements:

- Open with the specific finding or number, not a generic hook ("Ever wondered why..."). If the article has a benchmark result or a surprising observation, lead with it directly.
- State the hypothesis and what was actually found, in plain language, in no more than 4-5 short lines.
- Do not oversell the finding. If the result was mixed, negative, or narrower than it sounds, say so — credibility compounds, hype doesn't.
- No emojis, no "🧵", no engagement-bait phrasing ("This changed everything," "Nobody talks about this"). Write like the article itself: direct, specific, evidence-first.
- End with one sentence on why it matters in practice, then the link.
- Keep total length under 150 words. If it needs more room to be accurate, that's a signal the underlying article's finding is too complex for a single post — say so rather than padding it out.
