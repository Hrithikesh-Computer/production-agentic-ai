## Implemented evidence versus gaps

No row above is implemented by this document or by the repository's CRM prototype. The adaptive-response-filter HMAC authenticates message envelopes under a shared key, not the identities or decisions of this governance workflow.

Every layer remains `EVIDENCE REQUIRED` for a production governance agent: executable identity checks, action/resource policy, lineage records, derivation constraints, conflict precedence, runtime revalidation, intent evaluation, and fail-closed decision/approval tests. The exact policy and threat model require owner and compliance input.
